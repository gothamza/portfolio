# Interview speech — English (full script)

**Length:** ~6–8 minutes if spoken calmly.  
**How to use:** Read aloud once, then practice without looking. Adapt the closing to the company name when you know it.

---

Hello, and thank you for your time today.

My name is Hamza Bouktitiya. I am an Artificial Intelligence Engineer based in Kenitra, Morocco. I build AI systems that go beyond prototypes: language models, RAG pipelines, agents, APIs, and the interfaces people actually use — then I ship them with Docker and real deployment.

I will walk you through who I am, my education, my professional experience, a few projects that show my range.

I studied at Ibn Tofail University in Kenitra. I earned a Bachelor’s degree in Mathematical and Computer Science between 2018 and 2022, then a Master’s in Artificial Intelligence and Virtual Reality from 2022 to 2024. That path gave me a strong foundation in mathematics, software, and modern AI — from classical machine learning to deep learning, NLP, and computer vision.

Until October 2026, I worked as an AI Developer at Obystech Solutions in Rabat, from April 2025. In this role I did two things that go together. First, I helped enterprises understand where AI can create real value — machine learning, deep learning, and NLP — especially inside business processes like CRM, sales, inventory, and HR. Second, I designed and delivered the solutions myself, including assistants integrated with ERP systems such as Odoo, so users can trigger business actions in natural language instead of fighting complex menus.

A concrete example of that work is a multi-department AI agent platform I designed and shipped as a solo project for HR, Finance, Management, and IT. On the front end I built a Next.js multi-chat interface with per-chat RAG, team workspaces, and access controls. On the back end I used FastAPI and LangGraph: document ingestion, Hugging Face embeddings, hybrid search, MCP tools, and memory in PostgreSQL, with ChromaDB as the vector store. I secured the LLM APIs with typed schemas, JWT and OAuth2, and role-based access. Then I deployed everything as a multi-service Docker Compose stack on a VPS, with reverse proxy, SSL, and zero-downtime restarts. That project is a good picture of how I work: I own the problem from consulting and architecture all the way to production.

Before Obystech, I was an AI Intern at Verve Technologie in Casablanca, from February to August 2024. There I built an LSTM system to forecast energy consumption, exposed Stable Diffusion image-generation endpoints, and put a RAG pipeline over Wikipedia and PDF sources behind FastAPI with JWT authentication and user management. Even earlier, in 2022, I did a web development internship at the Computer Science department of Ibn Tofail University, where I built a gym management application with PHP, JavaScript, Bootstrap, and MySQL. Those experiences taught me to move from classical web apps, to ML services, to full LLM products.

Outside of work I keep shipping personal and client projects. One ongoing client project is Centre Souifi, a multilingual marketing site for an evening tutoring and languages centre — Arabic, French, and English, with RTL support, deployed on GitHub Pages. I also built an Intelligent Assessment System using LLMs and RAG for exam generation, answer evaluation, and feedback with metrics like BLEU, ROUGE, and BERTScore. I have a Computer Vision Playground with face detection, recognition, YOLOv8, and pose estimation in Streamlit and Docker. Earlier I worked on Darija–English question answering and a license-plate recognizer that reached about 98.9 percent test accuracy. Together, these projects show that I am comfortable across NLP, LLMs, vision, and product-facing web work.

My technical toolbox reflects that. On the AI side I use PyTorch, LangChain, LangGraph, RAG, NLP, and computer vision. On the backend: FastAPI, Python, PostgreSQL, JWT. On the frontend: Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind. For delivery: Docker, Nginx, VPS deployment, and Git. I speak Arabic natively, English at C1, and French at B2, which helps me work with local and international teams. I also stay active in the community: Top 3 at the DyslexAI hackathon at Al Akhawayn, ThinkAI at 1337, co-founder of the Pragnomos club, and an ML talk at an Agora event.

What I am looking for now is a role where I can keep building AI products end to end — especially around LLMs, RAG, agents, and applied NLP — inside a team that cares about shipping to production, about reliability, and about the people who use the product. I want to contribute quickly, own meaningful features, and keep learning from strong colleagues.

Thank you again for listening. I would be happy to answer your questions, or to go deeper on any project or technical topic you care about.
